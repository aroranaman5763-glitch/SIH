**1. Story Hunt (0:00, 15 s)**
Good morning. We are Story Hunt.
From Marina Bay to the Taj Mahal, our grandparents share one problem.
And we think a story can fix it.

**2. 1 in 3 feel lonely (0:15, 30 s)**
One in three older Singaporeans feels lonely. In India, it is the same: one in three.
This is not just sad. A Duke-NUS study found that a lonely 60-year-old can expect to live three to five years less.
Loneliness is a health problem.

**3. Not another app (0:45, 20 s)**
Most tech for seniors keeps them at home, on a screen.
We asked a different question: what would make a grandparent put on their shoes and go out?
Our answer: a story they cannot finish alone.

**4. One legend, four voices (1:05, 25 s)**
Story Hunt tells a legend as a comic, in four voices.
Our first one is how Singapura got its name: a prince of Chola descent, a Tamil sailor, a sea girl and a Chinese trader.
Each senior gets one character, and sees only one part of the truth.
To solve it, the four must meet.

**5. The journey (1:30, 20 s)**
They join, and read or listen for a few minutes a day.
We match four neighbours who share a skill.
They meet at a café, tap their card, and solve three questions together.
Then they give back, and earn rewards for their health.

**6. Live demo (1:50, 1:20)**
Let me show you. *(Play the video. Talk lightly over it, or let the captions speak.)*
*(When it ends:)* All of that is live today, at story-hunt-rho.vercel.app.

**7. Built for seniors (3:10, 20 s)**
It speaks their language: English, Chinese, Malay, Tamil and Hindi.
It reads aloud in natural voices, even Cantonese, and Hokkien and Teochew from volunteer recordings.
Big text. One button at a time. At the café, you just tap a card.

**8. Business model (3:30, 35 s)**
Seniors never pay.
Cafés pay us for each verified visit in their quiet afternoon hours. They fill empty tables.
Wellness partners fund the rewards: tai chi, yoga, health checks. They reach active seniors.
NGOs and schools receive circles of skilled volunteers.
And active-ageing grants can fund the rest.

**9. Impact and pilot (4:05, 30 s)**
Story Hunt touches all four parts of healthy ageing.
Social: four new friends. Mental: reading and reasoning. Purpose: teaching a skill. Physical: rewards only for fitness.
Our pilot: one café, one active ageing centre, ten circles, eight weeks. We will measure loneliness before and after.

**10. What's next (4:35, 25 s)**
Next, more legends: Badang the Strongman for Singapore, and Indian legends for our India launch.
We are looking for café, active ageing centre and NGO partners.
Story Hunt: a story that only ends when four people meet.
Thank you.
